# Approval Authority Matrix

PRC-004 sets what each of the five roles may view and approve: Customer Service Representative, Loan Officer, Underwriter, Senior Underwriter and Credit Committee. An Underwriter approves loans up to 750,000 and a Senior Underwriter up to 1,500,000.

## Roles in the authority matrix

Demo Mortgage Co. assigns credit authority to five roles, and a person acts only within the authority of the role they hold. The roles, from least to most authority, are the Customer Service Representative, the Loan Officer, the Underwriter, the Senior Underwriter and the Credit Committee. The Credit Committee is a group of senior credit staff named by Credit Risk, and it decides by the vote recorded in the minutes. Authority belongs to the role and not to the person, so a person who changes role takes on the authority of the new role from the date of the change. Authority is never delegated downward: if a Senior Underwriter is absent, a file in their authority goes to another Senior Underwriter or to the Credit Committee, and not to an Underwriter. A person may not approve a file they originated or in which they have a personal interest.

## What each role may view

Access follows least privilege under POL-011, so each role sees only the data it needs.

| Role | May view |
| --- | --- |
| Customer Service Representative | File status and the contact details needed to answer the customer; not the financial documents |
| Loan Officer | The application and supporting documents of the files they handle |
| Underwriter | The whole file of each file assigned to them |
| Senior Underwriter | The whole file of each file assigned to them or referred to them |
| Credit Committee | The file and the exception request put before it |

A request for wider access must go to the department that manages the system, with the approval of a manager. Access is removed when the role changes.

## What each role may approve

The Customer Service Representative approves nothing. The Loan Officer approves nothing, and may take an application, check that it is complete and recommend it to Underwriting. The Underwriter may approve a file that meets every policy, up to the loan amount limit below, and may prepare and recommend an exception but may not approve one. The Senior Underwriter may approve a file that meets every policy up to the higher loan amount limit, and may approve the exceptions listed in PRC-005 for a Senior Underwriter. The Credit Committee may approve any loan amount above the Senior Underwriter limit and may approve the exceptions that belong to it. Where a file needs both a credit approval and an exception, the higher of the two authorities decides who approves.

## Loan amount authority limits

Loan amount authority is set by the size of the loan. An Underwriter may approve a loan amount up to 750,000. A Senior Underwriter may approve a loan amount up to 1,500,000. Above 1,500,000 the file must go to the Credit Committee. For a home equity line of credit under HEL-1 the loan amount is the full credit limit. The amount is the total of all Demo Mortgage Co. mortgage debt that the borrower will have on the property after the loan is advanced, so a second loan to the same borrower is added to the first. The limits apply to a file that meets every policy. A file that needs an exception goes to the role named for that exception, which may be higher than the role for the loan amount.

## Exception approval authority

The authority to approve exceptions is the same as in POL-010 and is applied through PRC-005. A Senior Underwriter may approve a GDS or TDS exception of up to 2.0 points over the limit. The Credit Committee may approve a GDS or TDS exception of up to 4.0 points over the limit. A ratio more than 4.0 points over the limit cannot be excepted. A Senior Underwriter may approve an income-document exception and a rate hold extension. The Credit Committee approves a loan-to-value exception, and may waive the rate hold extension fee. An Underwriter approves no exception. Privacy rules, fraud flags, property eligibility, the borrowed down payment ban, the qualifying rate, the minimum down payment and the maximum amortization can never be excepted by any role.

## Example: routing three files

A file for a loan of 600,000 meets every policy. An Underwriter may approve it. A file for a loan of 1,100,000 meets every policy. It is above the Underwriter limit and within the Senior Underwriter limit, so a Senior Underwriter approves it. A file for a loan of 600,000 has a TDS ratio 3.0 points over the limit. The loan amount is within the Underwriter limit, but the exception is above the Senior Underwriter limit of 2.0 points, so the Credit Committee must approve the exception before the file can be approved. A file for a loan of 1,700,000 that meets every policy goes to the Credit Committee because of the loan amount, even though no exception is needed. In each case the Underwriter records the loan amount, the authority limit that applies and the role that approved the file in the loan system.
