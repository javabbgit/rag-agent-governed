# Home Equity Line of Credit (HEL-1)

PRD-005 describes HEL-1, the Demo Mortgage Co. home equity line of credit. It is priced at DMC Prime plus 0.75, has a maximum combined loan-to-value of 65%, requires interest-only minimum payments and is qualified at the qualifying rate.

## HEL-1 product summary

HEL-1 is the Demo Mortgage Co. home equity line of credit, and its product code is HEL-1. It is a revolving line of credit secured on the borrower's owner-occupied residential property, which must meet POL-006. The borrower may draw on the line up to the approved credit limit, repay, and draw again. HEL-1 has no fixed term and its interest rate moves with DMC Prime. Every HEL-1 application is tested at the qualifying rate under POL-004 and against the ratio limits in POL-003, and the approval authority is set by the credit limit (see PRC-004). An exception to any of these tests may be considered only as POL-010 allows. A borrower who wants HEL-1 together with a mortgage applies under PRC-001, and Underwriting decides both under PRC-002.

## HEL-1 rate and qualifying rate

The HEL-1 contract rate is DMC Prime plus 0.75 points. On the rate sheet effective 2026-11-01, DMC Prime is 4.70%, so the HEL-1 rate is 5.45% (see RATE-001). The rate changes whenever DMC Prime changes. Without a rate hold, the rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date. HEL-1 is qualified at the qualifying rate, like every other product. The qualifying rate for HEL-1 is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40%. At a contract rate of 5.45% the qualifying rate is 7.20%, because 5.45% plus 1.75 points is more than the 5.40% floor. The payment used in the GDS and TDS ratios is calculated at the qualifying rate on the full credit limit and not on the amount drawn, as POL-004 requires. The qualifying rate is a testing rate and is not the rate the borrower pays.

## HEL-1 combined loan-to-value limit

The maximum combined loan-to-value for HEL-1 is 65%. Combined loan-to-value is the balance of the first mortgage plus the full HEL-1 credit limit, divided by the property value, and the property value is the lower of the purchase price and the appraised value under POL-007. If the borrower has no first mortgage, the combined figure is the credit limit divided by the property value. The 65% limit is a maximum for HEL-1 and mortgage default insurance does not raise it. A full appraisal is required on every HEL-1 file (see POL-006). An approved credit limit may not be increased without a new application. A file that exceeds the combined limit needs an exception under POL-010 and may not be approved before the exception is approved.

## HEL-1 interest and minimum payment

Interest on HEL-1 is calculated daily on the drawn balance at the HEL-1 contract rate and is charged to the line each month, so it is compounded monthly. The minimum payment each month is the interest charged for the month, which is called an interest-only minimum payment. The minimum payment does not reduce the amount borrowed, so the balance is repaid only if the borrower pays more than the minimum. The minimum payment changes when DMC Prime changes or when the drawn balance changes. Mortgage Operations tells the borrower the minimum payment on each statement. A borrower who does not pay the minimum payment is in arrears, and a returned payment may carry the NSF fee in RATE-002.

## HEL-1 repayment and fees

A HEL-1 borrower may repay any part of the drawn balance at any time, and no prepayment penalty is charged. The lump-sum limit, the payment-increase limit and the penalty rules for the closed mortgage products in POL-009 do not apply to HEL-1. The only fees that may be charged on HEL-1 are those in RATE-002, and the discharge fee applies when the line is closed and the security is discharged. A borrower who closes the line pays the drawn balance and the interest to the date of closing. Staff must not tell a borrower that the credit limit can be increased or that a draw will always be available, because Underwriting decides every credit limit and the line may be reviewed.
