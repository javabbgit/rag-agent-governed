# Borrower FAQ: Renewing Your Mortgage

Answers to common questions about renewing your mortgage when its term ends: the notice you receive 120 days before maturity, your choices, whether you must qualify again, and what happens if you do nothing.

## When will I hear about renewing my mortgage?

Demo Mortgage Co. sends you a renewal notice in writing 120 days before your mortgage matures (see PRC-007). The notice goes to you, the borrower, and not only to your broker. It tells you your maturity date, the balance you will owe on that date, the choices you have, how to give your instruction and the date by which we must receive it. It also points you to the current rate sheet (see RATE-001). If you have not received a notice, ask Customer Care. Demo Mortgage Co. cannot tell you today what rate will be available when your mortgage matures, and staff may not promise a future rate. The renewal notice is the main way that Mortgage Operations tells you what happens next. The notice explains how to give your instruction.

## What can I choose when my mortgage matures?

When your mortgage matures, you can renew into a closed fixed-rate term: FX5-C for 5 years or FX3-C for 3 years. You can renew into the variable-rate mortgage VR5-C. You can renew into the open mortgage FX1-O. You can also pay off your mortgage in full, with no prepayment penalty at maturity, or you can refinance by making a new application (see PRC-007). Each renewal starts a new term, and your prepayment privileges and penalty start again on the first day of the new term (see POL-009). Mortgage Operations can explain each choice in plain words, but it cannot tell you which one is best for you, because that depends on your own plans.

## Do I have to qualify again to renew?

Not for a straight renewal. A straight renewal is a renewal of your existing Demo Mortgage Co. mortgage at maturity, with the same borrowers, for no more than the balance you owe, with no change to the property. A straight renewal is not requalified, so Demo Mortgage Co. does not test your income and debts again and does not ask for new income documents (see POL-004 and PRC-007). You can choose any renewal choice and it does not change this. You still need to give current proof of home insurance on the property. The time you have left to repay your mortgage cannot be made longer than the limits in POL-007. A straight renewal is still a credit decision, and Demo Mortgage Co. cannot promise it in advance.

## What if I want more money or to change who is on my mortgage?

If you ask for more money, add a borrower, remove a borrower or change the property, your request is not a straight renewal. It is treated as a new application (see PRC-007). Underwriting then tests the new application against the credit policies, including the qualifying rate in POL-004 and the ratio limits in POL-003, in the same way as for a new mortgage. Approval is not guaranteed. You cannot ask for a longer time to repay your mortgage as part of a renewal, because the limits in POL-007 apply and the time left may not be extended. If the new application is not approved, you can still renew your existing balance as a straight renewal, if that is what you choose.

## What happens if I do not tell you what I want?

If Demo Mortgage Co. has not received your instruction by your maturity date, your mortgage renews into the 1-year fixed open mortgage, FX1-O (see PRC-007). Your balance renews at the FX1-O rate on the rate sheet in force on your maturity date (see RATE-001). Mortgage Operations sends you the new terms in writing on the same business day. The FX1-O rate is higher than the rate of the closed terms, so staying in FX1-O may cost you more. You can move to a closed term at any time, and there is no penalty for moving because FX1-O has none (see PRD-004). This is why it is worth giving your instruction before your maturity date.

## What rate will I get when I renew?

Without a rate hold, the rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date. For a renewal, the funding date is the first day of your new term (see RATE-001). That rate may be different from the rate you see today, and Demo Mortgage Co. cannot promise a future rate. If your mortgage renews by default, you get the FX1-O rate on your maturity date. There is no fee for a renewal at maturity (see RATE-002). A rate hold is not an approval. If you would like to compare choices, the current rates are in the rate sheet RATE-001, and staff cite it when they quote a rate.
