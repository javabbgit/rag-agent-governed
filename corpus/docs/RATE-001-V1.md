# Rate Sheet (effective 2026-08-01)

Superseded on 2026-11-01 by RATE-001 version 2.0. RATE-001-V1 is the Demo Mortgage Co. rate sheet effective 2026-08-01. It lists DMC Prime, the contract rate of every product and the qualifying rate of every product.

## DMC Prime and the effective date

The rate sheet effective 2026-08-01 replaced the rate sheet before it. DMC Prime in RATE-001-V1 is 4.95%. DMC Prime is the reference rate set by Demo Mortgage Co. for its variable-rate products. The variable products, VR5-C and HEL-1, are priced as DMC Prime plus or minus a fixed spread, so their rates change whenever DMC Prime changes. The fixed-rate products have rates that are set directly on the rate sheet and do not move once a mortgage is advanced. Product terms, such as the term, the prepayment privileges and the loan-to-value limit, are in the PRD documents. Product owners at Demo Mortgage Co. review the rate sheet and issue a new one whenever the rates change. Staff must always check the effective date of a rate sheet before quoting a rate from it.

## Qualifying rate by product

The qualifying rate of each product is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40% (see POL-004). In RATE-001-V1 the contract rate plus 1.75 points is above the 5.40% floor for every product, so the floor does not apply to any product. Underwriting uses the figures in the table to test every application.

The qualifying rate is a testing rate and is never quoted to a borrower as the rate of a mortgage. For HEL-1 it is applied to the full credit limit.

## How rate holds and rate changes work

A rate hold locks the contract rate of a product for the hold period, so the borrower gets the held rate if the mortgage closes within that period (see RATE-003). Without a rate hold, a rate on the rate sheet may change at any time. Without a rate hold, the rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date. A new rate sheet replaces the earlier one on its effective date, and the earlier one becomes a superseded document. Staff must never promise that a rate in RATE-001-V1 will still be available later, and must never quote a rate beyond a rate hold (see PRC-009). A rate hold is not an approval. The qualifying rate for a file with a rate hold is calculated from the held rate.

## Fees, renewals and home equity rates

Fees are not set out in RATE-001-V1. They are in the fee schedule RATE-002, and a fee that is not listed there is not charged. A borrower who asks about a fee must be given the amount from RATE-002 and not from memory. The rate for a renewal is set on the same basis: the funding date of a renewal is the first day of the new term, and a renewal that happens by default uses the FX1-O rate on the maturity date (see PRC-007). Rates for home equity lines of credit change with DMC Prime in the same way as VR5-C, and the HEL-1 payment is recalculated under the rules in PRD-005. A rate sheet that has been replaced may be used only to understand past decisions.
