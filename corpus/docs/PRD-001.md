# 5-Year Fixed Closed (FX5-C)

PRD-001 describes the FX5-C mortgage: a 5-year closed fixed-rate term. Prepayment follows POL-009, and the penalty on a prepayment above the privileges is the greater of 3 months' interest or the interest rate differential.

## FX5-C product summary

The FX5-C mortgage is the Demo Mortgage Co. 5-year fixed closed mortgage, and its product code is FX5-C. The term is 5 years, the interest rate is fixed for the whole term, and the mortgage is closed, which means the borrower may prepay only as POL-009 allows. FX5-C is for an owner-occupied residential property that meets POL-006. A non-owner-occupied property is lent on under RNT-4 and not under FX5-C. The amortization may be up to 25 years for an insured mortgage and up to 30 years for an uninsured mortgage (see POL-007). The loan-to-value limit is the conventional limit in POL-007, and a loan above that limit needs mortgage default insurance. Every FX5-C application is tested at the qualifying rate under POL-004 and against the ratio limits in POL-003. An exception to any of these tests may be considered only as POL-010 allows.

## FX5-C rate and qualifying rate

The FX5-C contract rate on the rate sheet effective 2026-11-01 is 4.69% (see RATE-001). The rate is fixed from the day the mortgage is advanced until the maturity date, whatever happens to DMC Prime. A borrower who has a rate hold gets the held rate if the mortgage closes within the hold period (see RATE-003). Without a rate hold, the rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date. The qualifying rate for FX5-C is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40%. At a contract rate of 4.69% the qualifying rate is 6.44%, because 4.69% plus 1.75 points is more than the 5.40% floor. The qualifying rate is a testing rate and is not the rate the borrower pays. Fixed-rate interest on FX5-C is compounded semi-annually, as set out in POL-012. Staff must quote the contract rate only from the current rate sheet.

## FX5-C prepayment privileges

The FX5-C mortgage has two prepayment privileges each anniversary year, as set by POL-009. The lump-sum privilege allows the borrower to prepay up to 18% of the original principal in each anniversary year without penalty. The payment-increase privilege allows the borrower to raise the regular payment by up to 18% of the current regular payment without penalty. The borrower may use both privileges in the same anniversary year. A privilege that is not used in one anniversary year does not carry forward to the next, and the limits start again on each anniversary of the closing date and on the first day of a renewed term. Mortgage Operations tracks the amount prepaid for FX5-C in each anniversary year and tells the borrower the remaining amount on request.

## FX5-C prepayment penalty

A prepayment on FX5-C that is above the privileges in POL-009 is subject to a penalty. The penalty on FX5-C is the greater of 3 months' interest or the interest rate differential (IRD). Three months' interest is the interest on the amount prepaid for 3 months at the contract rate. The IRD is the difference between the FX5-C contract rate and the rate Demo Mortgage Co. could charge today on a term equal to the remaining term, applied to the amount prepaid for the remaining term. Mortgage Operations calculates both figures and charges the greater. The penalty applies to a full payout before maturity, to a prepayment above the privileges and to a refinance before maturity. There is no penalty on the payout of an FX5-C mortgage at its maturity date. The discharge fee in RATE-002 is charged separately.

## FX5-C payment frequencies

A borrower with an FX5-C mortgage may choose monthly, semi-monthly, bi-weekly, weekly, accelerated bi-weekly or accelerated weekly payments. Mortgage Operations calculates every FX5-C payment under POL-012, with fixed-rate interest compounded semi-annually, and rounds each payment to the cent. The accelerated frequencies are based on the monthly payment, so they repay the FX5-C mortgage faster than the matching standard frequencies. The borrower chooses the frequency on the application. A later change of payment frequency or payment date carries the payment change fee in RATE-002 and takes effect from the next payment date. A missed or returned payment may carry the NSF fee in RATE-002. Staff must tell the borrower which frequency is in the mortgage terms, and must not describe an accelerated frequency as a different product or as a way to avoid the prepayment limits in POL-009.

## FX5-C renewal at maturity

An FX5-C mortgage matures at the end of its 5-year term. Mortgage Operations sends the borrower a renewal notice 120 days before the maturity date (see PRC-007). The borrower may renew into any closed or open product, or repay the mortgage in full. A renewal of FX5-C with the same borrowers, for no more than the outstanding balance and with no change to the property, is a straight renewal and is not requalified (see POL-004). If the borrower gives no instruction by the maturity date, the mortgage renews into FX1-O. The prepayment privileges and penalty of the new term start on its first day. Mortgage Operations records whether the renewal was by instruction or by default, and keeps the renewal notice and the borrower's reply in the file.
