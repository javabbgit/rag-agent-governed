# Staff FAQ: Using the Policy Assistant

Answers for staff about the policy assistant: its answers come with sources, it says when no supporting document was found, it cannot submit or approve anything without a human, and you must never paste a SIN or account number into it.

## Where do the policy assistant's answers come from?

Every answer from the policy assistant comes with sources. The sources are the IDs of the Demo Mortgage Co. documents that support the answer, for example POL-003 for a ratio limit or PRC-005 for the exception procedure. Use the source IDs to open the document and read it yourself before you rely on the answer, and cite the policy ID when you pass the answer on (see PRC-009). Check that each source is a current document and not a superseded document whose ID ends in -V1, because a superseded document is kept only to understand past decisions (see POL-001). The policy assistant is a help for finding the right document, and the documents themselves are the rules.

## What does the policy assistant say when it is not sure?

When the policy assistant cannot find a document that supports an answer, it says that no supporting document was found. It does not guess. When you see that message, do not assume that the answer is no, or that the answer is yes. Refer the question to the department that owns the relevant policy (see POL-001), and wait for its answer. Until you have that answer, do not pass anything on to a borrower or broker as if it were a rule. If you think a document is missing or out of date, tell the owning department so that it can be corrected. A message to a borrower or broker must never state a rule for which you have no supporting document (see PRC-009).

## Can the policy assistant approve a file or submit a request?

No. The policy assistant cannot submit anything and cannot approve anything without a human. Approvals are decisions recorded by the role with authority under PRC-004, and an exception request stays pending until a human with the right authority approves it (see PRC-005). The policy assistant may help you find the rule or draft wording, but you must raise any form EXC-100 yourself, and an approver must decide it. A message that the policy assistant has drafted must also be reviewed by a human before it is sent (see PRC-009). Nothing the policy assistant says is an approval, and you must not tell a borrower or broker that it is.

## What must I never type into the policy assistant?

Never type or paste a SIN, a payment card number or a bank account number into the policy assistant, or into any other chat tool (see POL-011). Do not type a customer's date of birth, home address, telephone number, email address or employer details either. If you need to refer to a customer, use the loan number only, in the format DMC-YYYY-NNNNN. Describe the situation in general terms, such as the product, the ratios and the policy involved, and not the personal story of the borrower. If you see that customer data has been typed into a chat tool, by you or by anyone else, report it to Compliance on the same business day, giving the loan number and a short description but not the customer data itself.

## Can I send a message that the policy assistant drafted?

Only after a human has reviewed it. Every draft message must be reviewed before it is sent, whether you wrote it or the policy assistant drafted it (see PRC-009). Read the draft in full. Check that it makes no promise of approval, that it promises no rate beyond a rate hold, that any rate comes from the current rate sheet RATE-001, that it cites the right policy ID, that it is in plain language and that it contains no customer data beyond what is needed. For a decline or a complaint response, the reviewer must be a person with authority for the decision. Keep a copy of the reviewed message in the loan file.

## How do I know a document is current?

Each document shows its status in its front matter, and the status is either current or superseded. A current document is the rule that applies today. A superseded document begins with a sentence that names the date and the document that replaced it, and its ID ends in -V1 (see POL-001). Do not use a superseded document to decide a new file. If a borrower or broker quotes an older limit, check the current version before you answer, and cite the policy ID in your reply. Documents rank in this order: policy, then process, then FAQ. If an FAQ such as FAQ-006 disagrees with a policy or a process, the policy or the process wins, and you should report the difference to the owning department.
