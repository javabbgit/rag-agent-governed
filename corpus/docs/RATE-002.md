# Fee Schedule

RATE-002 is the Demo Mortgage Co. fee schedule: a discharge fee of $295, an NSF fee of $45, a payment change fee of $25 and a rate hold extension fee of $150. Fees that are not listed are not charged.

## Fees charged by Demo Mortgage Co.

The table lists every fee that Demo Mortgage Co. may charge on a mortgage or home equity line of credit.

| Fee | Amount | Charged when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discharge fee | $295 | The mortgage or line of credit is paid out and the security is discharged |
| NSF fee | $45 | A payment is returned because the account has insufficient funds |
| Payment change fee | $25 | The borrower asks to change the payment frequency or payment date |
| Rate hold extension fee | $150 | A rate hold is extended after an approved exception |

A staff member who is asked about a fee must read the amount from RATE-002 and must not quote it from memory. Mortgage Operations applies each fee only to the event shown in the table. The fee amounts in RATE-002 are the same for every product, and a product document that mentions a fee must give the same amount.

## When each fee is charged

The discharge fee is charged once, when a mortgage or line of credit is paid out in full or discharged, whether at maturity or earlier. It is charged in addition to any prepayment penalty that applies under POL-009. The NSF fee is charged when a payment is returned for insufficient funds, and it is charged each time a payment is returned. The payment change fee is charged when the borrower asks to change the payment frequency or the payment date after closing. It is not charged when Mortgage Operations changes the payment because DMC Prime has changed on a variable-rate mortgage. The rate hold extension fee is charged only when an extension has been approved under POL-010, as described in RATE-003. Each fee is shown on the borrower's statement or payout statement.

## Fees not listed are not charged

Demo Mortgage Co. charges only the four fees in RATE-002. A fee that is not listed is not charged. There is no fee for an application, for a renewal at maturity, for a conversion from a variable rate to a fixed term, for a prepayment within the privileges or for a statement. A prepayment penalty is not a fee. It is a charge under POL-009 and is described in the product documents. Staff must not invent a fee, must not charge a fee that is not listed and must not describe a charge as a fee in order to charge it. If a borrower is charged a fee that is not listed, Mortgage Operations must refund it and tell Customer Care.

## Waiving and correcting fees

Staff may not waive a fee. A fee may be removed only if it was charged in error, for example if an NSF fee was charged for a payment that was returned by mistake of Demo Mortgage Co. Mortgage Operations corrects the error, refunds the fee and records the reason in the file. The one fee that may be waived is the rate hold extension fee, and only by the Credit Committee as part of the decision on the exception (see POL-010 and PRC-005). A borrower who disputes a fee may complain under PRC-008, and the Complaints Office decides whether the fee was charged correctly. Staff must cite RATE-002 when they explain a fee to a borrower or broker.
