# Exception Request Procedure

PRC-005 sets how staff request an exception to credit policy. The request is made on form EXC-100, is routed by authority under POL-010, is decided within 2 business days, stays pending until a human approves it, and is never approved verbally.

## When an EXC-100 may be raised

An Underwriter raises an exception request on form EXC-100 when a file fails a test that POL-010 allows to be excepted. Only four things can be excepted: the GDS or TDS ratio limit, the loan-to-value limit, the income documents required by POL-002 or POL-008, and the rate hold period. Nothing else can be excepted. A request must not be raised for privacy rules, fraud flags, property eligibility, a borrowed down payment, the qualifying rate, the minimum down payment or the maximum amortization, and such a file must be declined or stopped. The Underwriter raises the request before the file is approved, not after. A Loan Officer or a broker may ask for an exception, but only an Underwriter may raise the EXC-100. The Underwriter must not tell the borrower that an exception will be granted.

## EXC-100 fields

The EXC-100 has five required fields, and a request is complete only when all five are filled in. The loan number gives the file in the format DMC-YYYY-NNNNN. The exception type names which of the four items is being excepted. The requested value states the figure being asked for, for example the ratio the file will have, the loan-to-value, the document that will be accepted in place of the required one or the new rate hold end date. The justification explains in plain words why the exception is sensible for this file. The compensating factors list the documented strengths of the file that reduce the risk, as defined in POL-010, and they must not already have been counted in the figure being excepted. The Underwriter attaches the supporting documents. An incomplete EXC-100 is sent back and the decision time does not start.

## Routing by authority

The Underwriter routes each EXC-100 to the role that has authority, using the table below. The routing is the same as the approval authority in POL-010 and PRC-004.

| Exception | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| GDS or TDS up to 2.0 points over the limit | Senior Underwriter |
| GDS or TDS more than 2.0 and up to 4.0 points over the limit | Credit Committee |
| GDS or TDS more than 4.0 points over the limit | Cannot be excepted |
| Loan-to-value | Credit Committee |
| Income documents | Senior Underwriter |
| Rate hold extension | Senior Underwriter |

Where both GDS and TDS are over their limits, the larger overage decides the route. A rate hold extension also carries the extension fee in RATE-002, which only the Credit Committee may waive. An Underwriter prepares and recommends the request but approves no exception.

## The 2-business-day decision time

The approver must decide a complete EXC-100 within 2 business days of receiving it. The count starts on the first business day after the EXC-100 is complete in the loan system. The approver records the decision as approved or refused, with the date, the reasons and the compensating factors relied on. If the approver needs more information, the approver returns the request to the Underwriter with the question, and the 2 business days start again when the answer is in the file. Credit Risk reports each period on the number of requests decided inside and outside the time. A request decided late is still valid, but the delay is recorded. Underwriting tells the broker or branch only that a review is under way, and never forecasts the result.

## Pending until a human approves

An exception request stays pending until a human with the right authority approves it. While it is pending, the file may not be approved on the failed test, the loan may not be advanced and no condition that depends on the exception may be signed off. The loan system keeps the file in a pending-approval status and does not release it on a timer, on silence or on the recommendation of the Underwriter. Nobody may give or accept a verbal approval, and a message or email without an approval recorded on the EXC-100 in the file has no effect. The approval applies only to the exception named on the EXC-100 and only to the facts described in it. If the loan amount, the property or the borrower changes, the approval lapses and a new EXC-100 is needed.

## Recording and closing the request

When the decision is made, the approver records it on the EXC-100, and the Underwriter files the EXC-100, the approval and the supporting documents in the loan file. If the exception is approved, the Underwriter continues the workflow in PRC-002 and records any condition set by the approver in the conditions list (see PRC-003). If it is refused, the Underwriter declines the file, or restructures it so that no exception is needed, and tells the broker or branch in plain words under PRC-009, citing the policy ID. A refused exception may not be raised again to a different approver for the same facts. The exception does not become a precedent, and staff may not cite an earlier exception as a reason to approve another one.
