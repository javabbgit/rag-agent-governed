# 3-Year Fixed Closed (FX3-C)

PRD-002 describes the FX3-C mortgage: a 3-year closed fixed-rate term with the same prepayment privileges as FX5-C. The penalty on a prepayment above the privileges is the greater of 3 months' interest or the interest rate differential.

## FX3-C product summary

The FX3-C mortgage is the Demo Mortgage Co. 3-year fixed closed mortgage, and its product code is FX3-C. The term is 3 years, the interest rate is fixed for the whole term, and the mortgage is closed, which means the borrower may prepay only as POL-009 allows. FX3-C is for an owner-occupied residential property that meets POL-006. A non-owner-occupied property is lent on under RNT-4 and not under FX3-C. The amortization may be up to 25 years for an insured mortgage and up to 30 years for an uninsured mortgage (see POL-007). The loan-to-value limit is the conventional limit in POL-007, and a loan above that limit needs mortgage default insurance. Every FX3-C application is tested at the qualifying rate under POL-004 and against the ratio limits in POL-003. An exception to any of these tests may be considered only as POL-010 allows.

## FX3-C rate and qualifying rate

The FX3-C contract rate on the rate sheet effective 2026-11-01 is 4.84% (see RATE-001). The rate is fixed from the day the mortgage is advanced until the maturity date, whatever happens to DMC Prime. A borrower who has a rate hold gets the held rate if the mortgage closes within the hold period (see RATE-003). Without a rate hold, the rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date. The qualifying rate for FX3-C is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40%. At a contract rate of 4.84% the qualifying rate is 6.59%, because 4.84% plus 1.75 points is more than the 5.40% floor. The qualifying rate is a testing rate and is not the rate the borrower pays. Fixed-rate interest on FX3-C is compounded semi-annually, as set out in POL-012. Staff must quote the contract rate only from the current rate sheet.

## FX3-C prepayment privileges

The FX3-C mortgage has the same two prepayment privileges as FX5-C each anniversary year, as set by POL-009. The lump-sum privilege allows the borrower to prepay up to 18% of the original principal in each anniversary year without penalty. The payment-increase privilege allows the borrower to raise the regular payment by up to 18% of the current regular payment without penalty. The borrower may use both privileges in the same anniversary year. A privilege that is not used in one anniversary year does not carry forward to the next, and the limits start again on each anniversary of the closing date and on the first day of a renewed term. Mortgage Operations tracks the amount prepaid for FX3-C in each anniversary year and tells the borrower the remaining amount on request.

## FX3-C prepayment penalty

A prepayment on FX3-C that is above the privileges in POL-009 is subject to a penalty. The penalty on FX3-C is the greater of 3 months' interest or the interest rate differential (IRD). Three months' interest is the interest on the amount prepaid for 3 months at the contract rate. The IRD is the difference between the FX3-C contract rate and the rate Demo Mortgage Co. could charge today on a term equal to the remaining term, applied to the amount prepaid for the remaining term. Mortgage Operations calculates both figures and charges the greater. The penalty applies to a full payout before maturity, to a prepayment above the privileges and to a refinance before maturity. There is no penalty on the payout of an FX3-C mortgage at its maturity date. The discharge fee in RATE-002 is charged separately.

## FX3-C payment frequencies

A borrower with an FX3-C mortgage may choose monthly, semi-monthly, bi-weekly, weekly, accelerated bi-weekly or accelerated weekly payments. Mortgage Operations calculates every FX3-C payment under POL-012, with fixed-rate interest compounded semi-annually, and rounds each payment to the cent. The accelerated frequencies are based on the monthly payment, so they repay the FX3-C mortgage faster than the matching standard frequencies. The borrower chooses the frequency on the application. A later change of payment frequency or payment date carries the payment change fee in RATE-002 and takes effect from the next payment date. A missed or returned payment may carry the NSF fee in RATE-002. Staff must tell the borrower which frequency is in the mortgage terms, and must not describe an accelerated frequency as a different product or as a way to avoid the prepayment limits in POL-009.

## Who FX3-C suits

FX3-C suits a borrower who wants the certainty of a fixed rate but expects their circumstances to change within 3 years, for example a borrower who may sell the property, who expects a change in income or who wants to review the choice of product sooner than a 5-year term allows. The shorter term means the borrower renews sooner and will then face the rate sheet in force at that time, which may be higher or lower. FX3-C has a higher contract rate than FX5-C on the current rate sheet (see RATE-001). Staff may describe these facts but may not tell a borrower which product is better for them, because that depends on the borrower's own plans. Renewal follows PRC-007, and a straight renewal is not requalified.
