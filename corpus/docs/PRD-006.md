# Rental Property Mortgage (RNT-4)

PRD-006 describes the RNT-4 mortgage: a 5-year closed fixed-rate term for non-owner-occupied 1-4 unit properties. The maximum loan-to-value is 75%, and 50% of gross rents is counted as income.

## RNT-4 product summary

The RNT-4 mortgage is the Demo Mortgage Co. rental property mortgage, and its product code is RNT-4. The term is 5 years, the interest rate is fixed for the whole term, and the mortgage is closed, which means the borrower may prepay only as POL-009 allows. RNT-4 is the only product that may be used for a non-owner-occupied property, which is a property the borrower will not live in (see POL-006). The amortization may be up to 25 years for an insured mortgage and up to 30 years for an uninsured mortgage (see POL-007). Every RNT-4 application is tested at the qualifying rate under POL-004 and against the ratio limits in POL-003. An exception to any of these tests may be considered only as POL-010 allows.

## RNT-4 rate and qualifying rate

The RNT-4 contract rate on the rate sheet effective 2026-11-01 is 5.09% (see RATE-001). The rate is fixed from the day the mortgage is advanced until the maturity date, whatever happens to DMC Prime. A borrower who has a rate hold gets the held rate if the mortgage closes within the hold period (see RATE-003). The qualifying rate for RNT-4 is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40%. At a contract rate of 5.09% the qualifying rate is 6.84%, because 5.09% plus 1.75 points is more than the 5.40% floor. The qualifying rate is a testing rate and is not the rate the borrower pays. Fixed-rate interest on RNT-4 is compounded semi-annually, as set out in POL-012.

## RNT-4 eligible properties

RNT-4 may be used for a non-owner-occupied residential property with 1-4 units that is located in Canada and meets POL-006. The property may be a one-unit home, a condominium unit or a property with up to four units. A full appraisal is required on every RNT-4 file. The Underwriter confirms at application that the borrower will not live in the property and records the answer in the file. If the borrower says the property will be owner-occupied, the file is not an RNT-4 file and is handled under another product. Property types that POL-006 lists as ineligible, such as vacant land, are also ineligible for RNT-4. The Underwriter also checks that the number of units in the appraisal agrees with the application and the leases, and refers any difference to the borrower before deciding.

## RNT-4 loan-to-value limit and rental income

The maximum loan-to-value for RNT-4 is 75%. The loan-to-value is calculated on the lower of the purchase price and the appraised value under POL-007, and mortgage default insurance does not raise the 75% limit. For income, Underwriting counts 50% of the gross rents of the property as income, as POL-002 requires, and the other 50% is not used anywhere in the ratio calculation. The borrower must supply a signed lease or rental agreement for each rental unit. A rental unit that is vacant at the time of the decision may be counted only if a signed lease starts before closing. A file that exceeds the RNT-4 limit needs an exception under POL-010 and may not be approved before the exception is approved. Rental income is added to the borrower's other income to give the income used in the ratios of POL-003.

## RNT-4 prepayment privileges and penalty

The RNT-4 mortgage has the same two prepayment privileges as FX5-C each anniversary year, as set by POL-009. The borrower may prepay up to 18% of the original principal in each anniversary year, and may raise the regular payment by up to 18% of the current regular payment, in both cases without penalty. Unused privileges do not carry forward. A prepayment on RNT-4 that is above the privileges is subject to a penalty. The penalty on RNT-4 is the greater of 3 months' interest or the interest rate differential (IRD), calculated by Mortgage Operations as for FX5-C. There is no penalty on the payout of an RNT-4 mortgage at its maturity date, and the discharge fee in RATE-002 is charged separately.

## RNT-4 payment frequencies and renewal

A borrower with an RNT-4 mortgage may choose monthly, semi-monthly, bi-weekly, weekly, accelerated bi-weekly or accelerated weekly payments, calculated under POL-012. Mortgage Operations sends the borrower a renewal notice 120 days before the maturity date (see PRC-007). A renewal of RNT-4 with the same borrowers, for no more than the outstanding balance and with no change to the property, is a straight renewal and is not requalified (see POL-004). If the borrower gives no instruction by the maturity date, the mortgage renews into FX1-O, as PRC-007 sets for every closed mortgage. The payment change fee in RATE-002 applies to a later change of frequency or date. Mortgage Operations records the frequency chosen and keeps the renewal notice in the file.
