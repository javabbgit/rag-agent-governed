# Exceptions to Credit Policy

POL-010 lists the items that can be excepted: GDS/TDS, LTV, income documents and rate hold extensions. A Senior Underwriter may approve up to 2.0 points over a ratio limit and the Credit Committee up to 4.0 points. Privacy rules and fraud flags are never exceptable.

## Items that can be excepted

Only four items in the Demo Mortgage Co. credit policy can be excepted. They are the GDS and TDS ratio limits in POL-003, the loan-to-value limits in POL-007, the income documents required by POL-002 and POL-008, and the rate hold period in RATE-003. Any rule that is not on that list cannot be excepted by any role. That includes the qualifying rate in POL-004, the minimum down payment and its sources in POL-005, the property types in POL-006 and the maximum amortization in POL-007. An exception changes the requirement for one file only. It does not change the policy, and it does not set a precedent for another file. Every exception must be requested on form EXC-100 and approved before the file is approved (see PRC-005). A request stays pending until a human with the right authority approves it.

## Items that can never be excepted

Some rules can never be excepted, whatever the compensating factors. Privacy rules can never be excepted: no role may approve the handling of customer data in a way that breaks POL-011. Fraud flags can never be excepted: a file with a fraud flag must be stopped and referred, and no one may approve it through the exception process. The borrowed down payment ban in POL-005 and the property eligibility rules in POL-006 are also not items that can be excepted, so a file that breaks them must be declined. Staff may not describe any of these as exceptions that may be possible, and may not raise an EXC-100 for them. Credit Committee has no authority to approve them either.

## Who can approve a GDS or TDS exception

Approval authority for a GDS or TDS exception depends on how far the ratio is over the limit in POL-003. The overage is the ratio minus its limit, measured in points, and where both ratios are over their limits the larger overage decides. A Senior Underwriter may approve an exception of up to 2.0 points over the limit. The Credit Committee may approve an exception of up to 4.0 points over the limit. An Underwriter may not approve any exception, but may prepare the request and recommend it. A ratio more than 4.0 points over the limit cannot be excepted, and the file must be declined or restructured. The role that approves must be named on the EXC-100 and must record the decision, the date and the reasons. The authority limits for loan amounts are separate and are set out in PRC-004.

## Who can approve LTV, income document and rate hold exceptions

A Senior Underwriter may approve an exception to the income documents required by POL-002 or POL-008, and an exception that extends a rate hold under RATE-003. An exception to the loan-to-value limit in POL-007 or to a product limit must be approved by the Credit Committee. A rate hold extension also needs the extension fee set out in RATE-002 unless the Credit Committee waives it. An approval is for the specific exception named on the EXC-100 and is valid only for the file and the facts described in the request. If the facts change, for example if the loan amount or the property changes, the approval lapses and the Underwriter must raise a new request.

## Compensating factors

An exception request must show compensating factors, which are documented strengths of the file that reduce the risk created by the exception. Examples are a long and stable employment history, a strong credit bureau record, a larger down payment than the minimum, substantial savings left after closing, low other debts and a long-standing relationship with Demo Mortgage Co. A compensating factor must be documented in the file and must not already have been counted in the ratio or the limit being excepted. A request with no compensating factors, or with factors that are vague or undocumented, must be sent back. The approver must record which factors were relied on. The Credit Committee may require more compensating factors for a larger exception than a Senior Underwriter would.

## Recording and timing of exceptions

The Underwriter must keep the EXC-100, the approval and the supporting documents in the file. An exception is not valid if it is given verbally, by message or by email without an EXC-100 approval in the file. Decisions on exception requests are due within 2 business days of a complete request. The request is complete when it names the loan number, the exception type, the requested value, the justification and the compensating factors (see PRC-005). Credit Risk reviews the exceptions approved in each period and reports to the Credit Committee. Staff may tell a borrower that an exception review is under way, but may not say that it will be approved. The Underwriter must also tell the broker or branch that a pending request is not a commitment to lend, and that the file may not go to closing until the approval is recorded in the file by the role named on the EXC-100.

## Example: which role approves which overage

A file has a GDS of 36.1% and a TDS of 39.4%. The GDS limit is 35%, so the GDS overage is 1.1 points. The TDS is under its limit. The larger overage is 1.1 points, so a Senior Underwriter may approve the exception. A second file has a TDS of 45.0%. The TDS limit is 42%, so the overage is 3.0 points, which is above 2.0 points and not above 4.0 points. The Credit Committee must approve it. A third file has a GDS of 39.5%. The overage is 4.5 points, which is above 4.0 points, so the file cannot be excepted and must be declined or restructured. For the first two files the Underwriter prepares the EXC-100 with the loan number, both ratios, the overage in points and the compensating factors, and sends it to the role that has authority for the overage, as described in PRC-005.
