# Borrower FAQ: Making Your Payments

Answers to common questions about making your mortgage payments: changing your payment date or how often you pay, what happens after a returned or missed payment, and the fees of $45 and $25 that can apply.

## Can I change my payment date or how often I pay?

Yes. After your mortgage has closed, you can ask Mortgage Operations at Demo Mortgage Co. to change your payment date or your payment frequency. The change takes effect from your next payment date. When you ask for a change of date or frequency, a payment change fee of $25 is charged (see RATE-002). You choose your frequency on your application, so you do not pay the fee for that first choice. The fee is not charged when your payment amount changes for another reason, for example when the interest rate on a variable-rate mortgage moves with DMC Prime. Your payments are calculated under POL-012, and each payment is rounded to the cent. If you are not sure which date or frequency suits you, ask Customer Care before you make the request, because a second change would carry the fee again.

## How often can I make my mortgage payments?

You can choose monthly payments, semi-monthly payments, bi-weekly payments, weekly payments, accelerated bi-weekly payments or accelerated weekly payments. Monthly means 12 payments a year, semi-monthly means 24, bi-weekly means 26 and weekly means 52. The two accelerated choices also mean 26 and 52 payments a year. An accelerated bi-weekly payment is half of your monthly payment, and an accelerated weekly payment is one quarter of your monthly payment (see POL-012). Because there are more payment dates in a year, accelerated payments pay your mortgage off faster than the matching standard choice. If you have a home equity line of credit, HEL-1, your minimum payment is the interest charged each month and is set out in PRD-005. Each payment is rounded to the cent.

## What happens if a payment is returned or missed?

If a payment is returned because your account does not have enough money in it, an NSF fee of $45 is charged each time a payment is returned (see RATE-002). RATE-002 lists no other fee for a returned or missed payment, and a fee that is not listed is not charged. If you have a home equity line of credit, HEL-1, and you do not pay the minimum payment, you are in arrears under the terms of PRD-005. If you think a fee was charged by mistake, tell Customer Care. A fee that was charged in error is corrected and refunded by Mortgage Operations. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can make a complaint, and Demo Mortgage Co. will acknowledge it within 2 business days and give you a final response within 30 days (see PRC-008).

## Will my payment change if I have a variable rate?

If you have a 5-year variable closed mortgage, VR5-C, your payment is recalculated whenever DMC Prime changes. Mortgage Operations works out the new payment from your balance, the new rate and the time left to repay your mortgage, using monthly compounding (see POL-012). The new payment starts on your next payment date after the change, and Demo Mortgage Co. tells you the new amount in writing. No payment change fee is charged for this. The time you have to repay your mortgage stays the same unless you ask for a change and Underwriting agrees. If DMC Prime falls, your payment falls, and if it rises, your payment rises. Demo Mortgage Co. cannot tell you what DMC Prime will do or promise that your payment will stay the same for the term. If you have a fixed-rate mortgage, your rate does not change during the term.

## Which fees can I pay on my mortgage payments?

Two fees can apply to your payments. The NSF fee of $45 is charged each time a payment is returned because there is not enough money in your account. The payment change fee of $25 is charged when you ask to change your payment frequency or your payment date after closing. These amounts come from the fee schedule in RATE-002. Demo Mortgage Co. charges only the fees in that schedule, so there is no fee for a statement, for a prepayment within your privileges or for a renewal at maturity. A discharge fee is charged when your mortgage is paid out and discharged. Staff may not waive a fee, but a fee charged by mistake is corrected and refunded. When you ask about a fee, staff cite RATE-002 so that you can check the amount yourself.
