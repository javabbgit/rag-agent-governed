# Qualifying Rate

This policy defines the qualifying rate used to test every application: the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40%. It applies to all products including HEL-1, and straight renewals are not requalified.

## How the qualifying rate is calculated

The qualifying rate is the greater of two figures: the contract rate plus 1.75 points, or 5.40%. The contract rate is the rate the borrower will actually pay on the mortgage. The 1.75 points are a buffer that tests whether the borrower could still afford the payments if rates rose. The 5.40% figure is a floor, so the qualifying rate is never lower than 5.40%, even when the contract rate is low. Underwriting must calculate both figures on every file and use whichever is greater. The qualifying rate is used to calculate the mortgage payment in the GDS and TDS ratios (see POL-003). It is a testing rate only. The borrower never pays it, it never appears in the mortgage terms, and staff may not quote it to a borrower or broker as the rate of the mortgage. Underwriting may not use a lower qualifying rate without an approved exception under POL-010.

## Products that must be qualified at the qualifying rate

The qualifying rate applies to all Demo Mortgage Co. products: FX5-C, FX3-C, VR5-C, FX1-O, RNT-4, SEA-5 and the home equity line of credit HEL-1. No product is tested at its contract rate alone. For a variable rate product such as VR5-C, the contract rate is DMC Prime plus or minus the product spread on the day of approval, and the qualifying rate is worked out from that figure. For HEL-1, the contract rate is DMC Prime plus 0.75, and the qualifying rate is calculated from it in the same way. For HEL-1 the payment used in the ratios is calculated at the qualifying rate on the full credit limit, not on the amount drawn. Product terms are kept in the PRD documents, and the current rates are kept in the rate sheet (see RATE-001).

## Example: qualifying rate for three products

Using the rate sheet effective 2026-11-01, DMC Prime is 4.70%. For FX5-C the contract rate is 4.69%. Contract rate plus 1.75 points is 6.44%, which is greater than 5.40%, so the qualifying rate is 6.44%. For VR5-C the contract rate is prime minus 0.55, which is 4.15%. Contract rate plus 1.75 points is 5.90%, which is greater than 5.40%, so the qualifying rate is 5.90%. For HEL-1 the contract rate is prime plus 0.75, which is 5.45%. Contract rate plus 1.75 points is 7.20%, so the qualifying rate is 7.20%. Now take a hypothetical contract rate of 3.50%. Contract rate plus 1.75 points is 5.25%, which is less than 5.40%, so the floor applies and the qualifying rate is 5.40%.

## Which contract rate is used to qualify

Underwriting must qualify the file at the contract rate the borrower will receive. If the borrower has a rate hold, the contract rate is the held rate, and the qualifying rate is worked out from it (see RATE-003). If there is no rate hold, the contract rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date (see RATE-001). Because the funding date is still ahead at the credit decision, Underwriting qualifies the file at the rate on the current rate sheet on the date of the decision and must recalculate if the rate changes before funding. If the contract rate changes before closing, for example because a rate hold ends or the borrower changes product, the Underwriter must recalculate the qualifying rate and both debt service ratios. A file that no longer meets the limits in POL-003 at the new qualifying rate may not close without an approved exception under POL-010. The Underwriter records the contract rate, both qualifying figures and the result in the file.

## Straight renewals are not requalified

A straight renewal is not tested at the qualifying rate. A straight renewal is a renewal of an existing Demo Mortgage Co. mortgage at maturity, with the same borrowers, for no more than the outstanding balance, and with no change to the property. The borrower may choose a new term or product, and Demo Mortgage Co. does not requalify the borrower for it. The renewal is still a credit decision and is handled under PRC-007. A renewal is not a straight renewal if the borrower asks for additional money, adds or removes a borrower, or changes the property. In those cases Underwriting must treat the request as a new application and apply the qualifying rate in POL-004. Staff must not tell a borrower that a renewal is guaranteed or that no review takes place.
