# Prepayment Privileges and Penalties

Superseded on 2026-06-01 by POL-009 version 2.0. POL-009-V1 set the prepayment privileges and penalties for Demo Mortgage Co. mortgages: a 15% lump sum per anniversary year, a payment increase of up to 15%, and a penalty on closed terms. FX1-O has no penalty.

## Lump-sum prepayment limit

The lump-sum privilege of a closed Demo Mortgage Co. mortgage allows the borrower to prepay up to 15% of the original principal in each anniversary year without penalty. The closed products are FX5-C, FX3-C, VR5-C, SEA-5 and RNT-4. An anniversary year runs from one anniversary of the closing date to the next. The limit is calculated on the original principal of the mortgage, not on the current balance. The borrower may make the lump sum in one payment or in several payments during the anniversary year, and the total may not exceed the limit. A prepayment above the limit is subject to the penalty in POL-009-V1 on the amount above the limit. Mortgage Operations tracks the amount prepaid in each anniversary year and tells the borrower the remaining amount on request.

## Payment increase limit

The payment-increase privilege of a closed Demo Mortgage Co. mortgage allows the borrower to raise the regular payment by up to 15% of the current regular payment without penalty. The increase may be made once in each anniversary year, and it takes effect from the next payment date after Mortgage Operations receives the request. A larger increase is not allowed, and Mortgage Operations may not accept it as a prepayment. The borrower may keep the higher payment for the rest of the term. A payment increase shortens the amortization because more of each payment reduces the principal. The payment-increase limit applies separately from the lump-sum limit, so the borrower may use both in the same anniversary year. The fee for changing a payment amount or date is in RATE-002.

## Prepayment penalty on a fixed-rate mortgage

A prepayment above the privileges on a closed fixed-rate mortgage is subject to a penalty. The closed fixed-rate products are FX5-C, FX3-C, SEA-5 and RNT-4. The penalty is the greater of 3 months' interest or the interest rate differential (IRD). Three months' interest is the interest on the amount prepaid for 3 months at the contract rate. The IRD is the difference between the contract rate and the rate that Demo Mortgage Co. could charge today on a mortgage with a term equal to the remaining term, applied to the amount prepaid for the remaining term. Mortgage Operations calculates both figures and charges the greater. The penalty applies to a full payout, to a prepayment above the privileges, and to a refinance of the mortgage before maturity. It is shown on the payout statement and is not waived by staff without an approved exception.

## Prepayment penalty on a variable-rate mortgage

A prepayment above the privileges on a closed variable-rate mortgage is subject to a penalty of 3 months' interest. The closed variable-rate product is VR5-C. Three months' interest is the interest on the amount prepaid for 3 months at the contract rate on the date of the prepayment. There is no IRD charge on a variable-rate mortgage. The lump-sum and payment-increase privileges in POL-009-V1 apply to VR5-C in the same way as to the fixed-rate products. A VR5-C borrower who converts to a fixed term follows the privileges and the penalty of the fixed product from the conversion date (see PRD-003). The home equity line of credit HEL-1 has its own repayment terms in PRD-005.

## FX1-O: no prepayment penalty

The open product FX1-O may be prepaid in full or in part at any time without penalty (see PRD-004). The lump-sum limit, the payment-increase limit and the penalty rules for closed products do not apply to FX1-O. Mortgage Operations does not charge a penalty on the payout of FX1-O. The borrower pays the interest to the payout date and, where applicable, the discharge fee in RATE-002. Staff may not describe the other products as having the same freedom. FX1-O has a higher rate than the closed terms in exchange for that freedom (see RATE-001). A borrower who asks about prepaying FX1-O should be told that no privilege limit applies, that no penalty is charged, and that the payout amount is shown on the payout statement prepared by Mortgage Operations.

## Unused privileges do not carry forward

A prepayment privilege that is not used in one anniversary year is lost at the end of that year and does not carry forward to the next. The 15% lump-sum limit and the 15% payment-increase limit are both reset on each anniversary of the closing date. Staff may not add an unused amount from a previous year to the current year's limit. A borrower who prepays only a small amount in the first year still has the same limit in the second year, and not a larger one. Mortgage Operations records the anniversary date for each mortgage and applies the new limits from that date. The same rule applies after a renewal: the privileges start again on the first day of the new term and are calculated on the principal that applies to the renewed mortgage, as set out in the renewal terms.

## Example: privileges and penalty on an FX5-C mortgage

A borrower has an FX5-C mortgage with an original principal of 400,000 and a regular payment of 2,000. The lump-sum limit is 15% of 400,000, which is 60,000 in each anniversary year. The payment may be raised by up to 15% of 2,000, which is 300, to 2,300. Suppose the borrower prepays 30,000 above the privileges when the balance is 300,000 and the contract rate is 4.89%. Three months' interest on 30,000 is 30,000 x 4.89% / 4 = 366.75. If the interest rate differential on 30,000 for the remaining term is 410.00, the penalty is 410.00, because it is the greater figure. If the borrower had instead prepaid only 45,000 in the anniversary year as a lump sum, no penalty would apply, because 45,000 is below the 60,000 limit. Mortgage Operations would record 45,000 as used and 15,000 as unused, and the unused 15,000 would be lost at the next anniversary.
