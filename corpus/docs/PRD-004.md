# 1-Year Fixed Open (FX1-O)

PRD-004 describes the FX1-O mortgage: a 1-year open fixed-rate term that may be prepaid in any amount at any time without penalty. It carries a higher rate than the closed terms and is the default product at maturity under PRC-007.

## FX1-O product summary

The FX1-O mortgage is the Demo Mortgage Co. 1-year fixed open mortgage, and its product code is FX1-O. The term is 1 year, the interest rate is fixed for the whole term, and the mortgage is open, which means the borrower may prepay at any time as described below. FX1-O is offered on a new application for an owner-occupied residential property that meets POL-006. A new application on a non-owner-occupied property is lent on under RNT-4 and not under FX1-O, but a closed mortgage of any kind that renews by default renews into FX1-O (see PRC-007). The amortization may be up to 25 years for an insured mortgage and up to 30 years for an uninsured mortgage (see POL-007). The loan-to-value limit is the conventional limit in POL-007, and a loan above that limit needs mortgage default insurance. Every FX1-O application is tested at the qualifying rate under POL-004 and against the ratio limits in POL-003. An exception to any of these tests may be considered only as POL-010 allows.

## FX1-O rate and qualifying rate

The FX1-O contract rate on the rate sheet effective 2026-11-01 is 6.99% (see RATE-001). It is higher than the contract rate of every closed term, because the borrower pays for the freedom to prepay without penalty. The rate is fixed from the day the mortgage is advanced until the maturity date, whatever happens to DMC Prime. Without a rate hold, the rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date. The qualifying rate for FX1-O is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40%. At a contract rate of 6.99% the qualifying rate is 8.74%, because 6.99% plus 1.75 points is more than the 5.40% floor. The qualifying rate is a testing rate and is not the rate the borrower pays. Fixed-rate interest on FX1-O is compounded semi-annually, as set out in POL-012. A borrower who needs FX1-O only briefly should be told that the rate is higher than the closed terms.

## FX1-O prepayment without penalty

An FX1-O borrower may prepay any amount at any time without penalty. The borrower may repay part of the balance, repay all of it, or raise the regular payment by any amount, on any date. The lump-sum limit, the payment-increase limit and the penalty rules for closed products in POL-009 do not apply to FX1-O. Mortgage Operations does not charge a penalty on the payout of an FX1-O mortgage, whether the payout is made during the term or at maturity. The borrower pays the interest to the payout date and, on a discharge, the discharge fee in RATE-002. Staff may not describe any closed product as having the same freedom. A borrower who wants to move from FX1-O to a closed term during the term may ask Mortgage Operations to arrange it, and no penalty is charged for the move.

## FX1-O as the default product at maturity

FX1-O is the product into which a mortgage renews when the borrower gives no instruction by the maturity date (see PRC-007). Mortgage Operations sends a renewal notice 120 days before maturity, and the notice tells the borrower that silence means renewal into FX1-O. The balance then renews for the FX1-O term at the FX1-O rate on the rate sheet in force on the maturity date (see RATE-001). The payment is recalculated under POL-012. Because FX1-O has a higher rate than the closed terms, Mortgage Operations does not describe the default as a benefit and tells the borrower in the new terms that a closed term may be chosen at any time. A renewal into FX1-O by default is a straight renewal if the borrowers, the balance and the property are unchanged, and it is not requalified.

## Who FX1-O suits

FX1-O suits a borrower who expects to repay the mortgage in full or in large part within a short time, for example from the sale of a property or from a payment the borrower expects to receive, and who values the freedom to do so without penalty over a lower rate. It also suits a borrower who is waiting to choose a closed term and wants to avoid committing at the current rates. Staff may describe these facts but may not recommend FX1-O or any other product, because the choice depends on the borrower's own plans. Payment frequencies for FX1-O are the same as for the closed fixed products, and the payment change fee in RATE-002 applies to a change of frequency or date.
