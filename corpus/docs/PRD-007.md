# Self-Employed Advantage Mortgage (SEA-5)

PRD-007 describes the SEA-5 mortgage: a 5-year closed fixed-rate term for self-employed borrowers with at least 2 years in business. The maximum loan-to-value is 75%, and income is verified under POL-008.

## SEA-5 product summary

The SEA-5 mortgage is the Demo Mortgage Co. Self-Employed Advantage Mortgage, and its product code is SEA-5. The term is 5 years, the interest rate is fixed for the whole term, and the mortgage is closed, which means the borrower may prepay only as POL-009 allows. SEA-5 is for an owner-occupied residential property that meets POL-006. A non-owner-occupied property is lent on under RNT-4 and not under SEA-5. The amortization may be up to 25 years for an insured mortgage and up to 30 years for an uninsured mortgage (see POL-007). Every SEA-5 application is tested at the qualifying rate under POL-004 and against the ratio limits in POL-003. An exception to any of these tests may be considered only as POL-010 allows.

## SEA-5 rate and qualifying rate

The SEA-5 contract rate on the rate sheet effective 2026-11-01 is 5.19% (see RATE-001). The rate is fixed from the day the mortgage is advanced until the maturity date, whatever happens to DMC Prime. A borrower who has a rate hold gets the held rate if the mortgage closes within the hold period (see RATE-003). Without a rate hold, the rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date. The qualifying rate for SEA-5 is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40%. At a contract rate of 5.19% the qualifying rate is 6.94%, because 5.19% plus 1.75 points is more than the 5.40% floor. The qualifying rate is a testing rate and is not the rate the borrower pays. Fixed-rate interest on SEA-5 is compounded semi-annually, as set out in POL-012.

## SEA-5 eligibility for self-employed borrowers

SEA-5 is for a self-employed borrower, as defined in POL-008. The borrower must have been in business for at least 2 years before the application, and Underwriting counts income as the 2-year average of net income shown on the notices of assessment. The borrower must supply the documents that POL-008 lists. A self-employed borrower who does not meet the 2-year rule may not use self-employed income to qualify for SEA-5 or for any other product. A self-employed borrower is not required to choose SEA-5 and may apply for any product for which they qualify, with the same income rules. The Underwriter records in the file why SEA-5 or another product was chosen, and the file shows the start date of the business and the two years of net income used.

## SEA-5 loan-to-value limit

The maximum loan-to-value for SEA-5 is 75%. The loan-to-value is calculated on the lower of the purchase price and the appraised value under POL-007, and a full appraisal is required on every SEA-5 file (see POL-006). Mortgage default insurance does not raise the 75% limit, so a borrower using SEA-5 may not exceed it by insuring the loan. A borrower who needs a higher loan-to-value must choose another product and meet that product's rules, and the same income rules in POL-008 still apply. A file that exceeds the SEA-5 limit needs an exception under POL-010 and may not be approved before the exception is approved. The Underwriter records the loan amount, the value used and the resulting loan-to-value in the file.

## SEA-5 prepayment privileges and penalty

The SEA-5 mortgage has the same two prepayment privileges as FX5-C each anniversary year, as set by POL-009. The borrower may prepay up to 18% of the original principal in each anniversary year, and may raise the regular payment by up to 18% of the current regular payment, in both cases without penalty. Unused privileges do not carry forward. A prepayment on SEA-5 that is above the privileges is subject to a penalty. The penalty on SEA-5 is the greater of 3 months' interest or the interest rate differential (IRD), calculated by Mortgage Operations as for FX5-C. There is no penalty on the payout of an SEA-5 mortgage at its maturity date, and the discharge fee in RATE-002 is charged separately.

## SEA-5 payment frequencies and renewal

A borrower with an SEA-5 mortgage may choose monthly, semi-monthly, bi-weekly, weekly, accelerated bi-weekly or accelerated weekly payments, calculated under POL-012. Mortgage Operations sends the borrower a renewal notice 120 days before the maturity date (see PRC-007). A renewal of SEA-5 with the same borrowers, for no more than the outstanding balance and with no change to the property, is a straight renewal and is not requalified (see POL-004), and the income documents of POL-008 are not requested again. If the borrower gives no instruction by the maturity date, the mortgage renews into FX1-O. The payment change fee in RATE-002 applies to a later change of frequency or date. Mortgage Operations records the frequency chosen and keeps the renewal notice in the file.
