# Complaint Handling and Escalation

PRC-008 sets how Demo Mortgage Co. handles complaints. Staff acknowledge a complaint within 2 business days and give a final response within 30 days. The path runs from the front line to the Complaints Office and then to an external complaints body.

## What counts as a complaint

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction by a borrower or applicant about a Demo Mortgage Co. product, service, staff member or decision, made in writing or by telephone, and one that the person wants to have looked into. The person does not need to use the word complaint. A question about a policy, or a request for information, is not a complaint unless the person says they are unhappy with the answer or the service. A complaint may come through a broker, a branch, Customer Care or any other contact, and the staff member who receives it must treat it as a complaint at once. A staff member may not discourage a person from complaining, and may not ask a person to withdraw one. A disagreement with a credit decision is a complaint, and the person may also ask for the decision to be reviewed under the exception rules only if POL-010 allows it.

## Front-line handling and recording

The staff member who receives a complaint is the front line. The front line must record the complaint in the loan system the same business day, using the loan number and not the name of the customer in any chat tool (see POL-011). The record states the date, the channel, the subject and what the customer wants. The front line tells the customer that the complaint has been recorded and will be acknowledged in writing. If the front line can resolve the matter on the spot, for example by correcting a mistake in a payment, the front line records the resolution and tells the customer. A resolution on the spot does not remove the duty to acknowledge. The front line must not promise an outcome, must not blame the customer or a colleague, and must cite the policy ID when explaining a decision.

## Acknowledgement within 2 business days

Every complaint must be acknowledged in writing within 2 business days of receipt. The acknowledgement confirms that the complaint was received, gives the loan number, names the department that is handling it and says when the customer can expect a final response. It does not state the outcome and does not admit or deny fault. Customer Care sends the acknowledgement for complaints received by the front line, and the Complaints Office sends it for complaints it receives directly. The 2 business days start on the first business day on which any staff member receives the complaint. If the complaint is received by a broker, the broker must pass it to Demo Mortgage Co. on the same business day.

## Escalation to the Complaints Office

The front line must escalate a complaint to the Complaints Office if it cannot be resolved on the spot, if the customer asks for escalation, if it concerns a credit decision, or if it alleges a breach of privacy or unfair treatment. The Complaints Office is the unit within Compliance that investigates complaints. It reads the loan file, speaks to the staff involved, checks the policy that applies and decides whether the complaint is upheld. The Complaints Office may ask Underwriting or Mortgage Operations for information, and those departments must answer promptly. The Complaints Office has no authority to approve an exception to credit policy, and any change of credit decision must go through POL-010 and PRC-005. It records every step in the complaint file.

## Final response within 30 days

The customer must receive a final response in writing within 30 calendar days of the date the complaint was received. The final response states what was investigated, the decision, the reasons and any action Demo Mortgage Co. will take. It also tells the customer that, if the customer is not satisfied, the customer may take the complaint to an external complaints body, and it explains how to do so. If the investigation cannot be completed in 30 days, the Complaints Office must write to the customer before the deadline, give the reason and the new date. The final response is sent by the Complaints Office and is reviewed by a human before it goes out, as required by PRC-009.

## The external complaints body

If a customer is not satisfied with the final response, or does not receive one within 30 days, the customer may take the complaint to an external complaints body. Demo Mortgage Co. must tell the customer this in the final response. Staff must not discourage a customer from going to the external body, must not argue about whether it can take the case and must not delay passing on the complaint file if the body asks for it. Compliance is the only department that deals with the external complaints body. The front line, Customer Care and Underwriting must send any contact from it to the Complaints Office on the same business day. Compliance reports each period to Credit Risk on the complaints received and the lessons learned.
