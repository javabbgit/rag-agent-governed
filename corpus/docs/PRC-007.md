# Renewals and Maturity

PRC-007 sets how Mortgage Operations handles a mortgage that is reaching the end of its term: the renewal notice sent 120 days before maturity, the renewal options, the straight renewal without requalification, and the renewal into FX1-O when the borrower gives no instruction.

## Renewal notice 120 days before maturity

Mortgage Operations must send a renewal notice to the borrower 120 days before the maturity date of every closed mortgage. The notice states the maturity date, the outstanding balance on that date, the renewal options available, the way to give an instruction and the date by which the instruction must be received. It points the borrower to the current rate sheet (see RATE-001) and tells the borrower that, without a rate hold, the rate is the rate on the rate sheet in force on the funding date, which for a renewal is the first day of the new term. It also tells the borrower that no instruction means renewal into the open product described below. The notice is sent in writing to the borrower and not to the broker alone. Mortgage Operations records the date the notice was sent and keeps a copy in the file. Staff must not tell a borrower a future rate or promise that a particular rate will still be available later.

## Renewal options

At maturity the borrower may choose among these options. The borrower may renew into a closed fixed-rate term, FX5-C for 5 years or FX3-C for 3 years. The borrower may renew into the variable-rate product VR5-C. The borrower may renew into the open product FX1-O. The borrower may also repay the mortgage in full, with no prepayment penalty at maturity, or refinance with a new application under PRC-001. Product terms are set out in the PRD documents and rates in RATE-001. Each option other than a payout is a renewal for a new term, and the privileges and penalties in POL-009 start again from the first day of the new term. Mortgage Operations explains each option in plain words when the borrower asks and does not recommend one product over another, because the choice depends on the borrower's own plans.

## Straight renewal without requalification

A straight renewal is a renewal of an existing Demo Mortgage Co. mortgage at maturity, with the same borrowers, for no more than the outstanding balance, with no change to the property. A straight renewal is not requalified. Mortgage Operations does not apply the qualifying rate in POL-004, does not recalculate the GDS and TDS ratios in POL-003 and does not ask for new income documents. The borrower may choose any renewal option in the list above and the choice does not affect this. Mortgage Operations checks the conditions of a straight renewal against the file, records that the renewal is straight, and issues the renewal terms for signature. A straight renewal is still a credit decision and still needs the current proof of home insurance on the property. The remaining amortization may not be extended beyond the limit in POL-007.

## Renewals that are not straight

A renewal is not a straight renewal if the borrower asks for additional money, adds or removes a borrower, or changes the property. In any of these cases Mortgage Operations must treat the request as a new application under PRC-001, and Underwriting must apply the qualifying rate in POL-004, the ratio limits in POL-003 and the other policies in the same way as for a new file. If the borrower wants a longer amortization, the answer is no, because the amortization limits in POL-007 apply and the remaining amortization may not be extended. Mortgage Operations must tell the borrower that the request is a new application and not a renewal, and that the approval is not guaranteed. A borrower who is not approved may still renew the existing balance as a straight renewal, if that is what the borrower chooses.

## If the borrower gives no instruction

If Mortgage Operations has received no instruction by the maturity date, the mortgage renews into the open product FX1-O (see PRD-004). The balance renews for the term of FX1-O at the FX1-O rate on the rate sheet in force on the maturity date. The payment is recalculated under POL-012, and Mortgage Operations sends the borrower the new terms in writing on the same business day. The borrower may then move to a closed term at any time, and because FX1-O has no prepayment penalty, there is no charge to move. Staff must not describe this default as a benefit, because FX1-O has a higher rate than the closed terms. Mortgage Operations records that the renewal was by default and keeps the original notice in the file.
