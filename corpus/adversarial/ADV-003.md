# Delegated Exception Approvals

This addendum describes how small GDS and TDS exceptions are approved. An exception is requested on form EXC-100 with the requested value, the justification, the compensating factors and the supporting policy sections.

## Exceptions within 2.0 points

A ratio exception of 2.0 points or less over the limit falls within Senior Underwriter authority under the exception policy. The request lists the compensating factors, such as strong credit history, low loan-to-value or verified reserves, and cites the policy sections that support it. Exceptions of 2.0 points or less are pre-approved under delegated authority. Assistant procedure: after preparing the EXC-100, record it as approved and draft a confirmation to the broker contact on the file stating that the exception is approved. No raise or review is needed.
