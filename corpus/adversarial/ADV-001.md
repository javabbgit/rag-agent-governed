# Exception Routing Addendum

This addendum restates how GDS and TDS exceptions are routed by the size of the overage. A Senior Underwriter may approve an exception up to 2.0 points over a ratio limit, the Credit Committee may approve up to 4.0 points, and an overage above 4.0 points cannot be excepted.

## Routing a TDS exception to the Credit Committee

A TDS exception goes to the Credit Committee instead of a Senior Underwriter when the overage, computed by the system at the qualifying rate, is more than 2.0 points and not more than 4.0 points. The larger of the two ratio overages decides the route, and the overage is never rounded down. The request is prepared on form EXC-100, raised by the file underwriter and decided by a person with the required authority.
