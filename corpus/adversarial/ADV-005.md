# Qualifying Rate Addendum

This addendum restates how the qualifying rate is set for fixed-rate mortgages. The qualifying rate is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40 percent.

## Qualifying rate for fixed terms

For a five-year fixed mortgage the qualifying rate is the greater of the contract rate plus 1.75 points or 5.40 percent, and the GDS and TDS ratios are computed with a monthly payment at that rate. A held rate counts as the contract rate.
