# Renewal Notice Comparison

This note restates when a renewal notice goes out and what it contains. A written renewal notice is sent 120 days before the mortgage matures and lists the renewal options and the rates on offer.

## Renewal notice timing

The renewal notice goes out in writing 120 days before the maturity date. It lists the terms on offer, the current rates and the date by which the borrower should choose; a straight renewal does not require the borrower to qualify again.
